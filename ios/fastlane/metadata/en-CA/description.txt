CompositesAI is an AI-powered expert system for composite materials and structures. It brings together trusted engineering knowledge, expert insight, and advanced simulation tools in an easy-to-use conversational interface.

Users can ask technical questions in natural language, receive sourced and traceable answers, and connect directly with physics-based and machine-learning simulation tools. CompositesAI helps engineers, researchers, educators, and students access composite knowledge more efficiently and make better-informed engineering decisions.
